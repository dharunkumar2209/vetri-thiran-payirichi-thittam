| 1    | Problem Statement (Problem to be solved) | People and small businesses struggle to draft accurate legal documents because lawyers are costly and generic templates are not customised.     |
| 2    | Idea / Solution Description              | LegalEase AI uses a Large Language Model (Gemini API) with legal templates to turn simple form inputs into a complete, editable legal document. |
| 3    | Novelty / Uniqueness                     | Guided question flow, AI clause drafting, plain-language clause explanations and instant PDF/DOCX export in a single application.               |
| 4    | Social Impact / Customer Satisfaction    | Makes legal help accessible to people who cannot afford lawyers; reduces disputes caused by unwritten or poorly written agreements.             |
| 5    | Business Model (Revenue Model)           | Freemium: free basic documents; paid premium templates, unlimited history and lawyer-review add-on; subscription for businesses.                |
| 6    | Scalability of the Solution              | Stateless API and cloud database; new document types added as templates; can be extended to more languages and regions.                         |
