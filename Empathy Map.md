## Empathy Map Canvas

**User Persona:** Small business owner / freelancer who needs a legal document quickly and cheaply.

| Quadrant | Details                                                                                                                                   |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Says     | "I need an agreement today." / "Lawyer fees are too high for a small deal." / "I don't understand this legal language."                   |
| Thinks   | "Will this document protect me?" / "Am I missing an important clause?" / "Is there an easy way to do this myself?"                        |
| Does     | Searches for free templates online, copies clauses from old documents, asks friends for help, visits a lawyer only when unavoidable       |
| Feels    | Anxious about legal risk, confused by jargon, frustrated by delays, relieved when the process is simple                                   |
| Pains    | High lawyer cost, complex legal language, generic templates, risk of missing clauses, time-consuming manual editing                       |
| Gains    | Instant customised documents, plain-language guidance, affordable access, editable output, download as PDF/DOCX, confidence in the result |
